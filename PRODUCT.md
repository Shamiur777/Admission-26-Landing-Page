# Product

Facts below come from the owner's brief and the existing project. Anything marked (inferred) was filled in without a live interview, because this run had no way to ask the owner.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and ES modules. No build step, no framework. Strict CSP, no inline script or style. Hosted as a static folder on edgecoursebd.com.

## Users

HSC students in Bangladesh preparing for university admission (inferred: age 17 to 19, mostly low and mid Android phones, arriving from a boosted Facebook ad inside the Facebook in-app browser on mobile data). Bangla first, English for course names.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Edge Course x Hulkenstein admission courses (24) and QNA Publication admission books (18). Visitors should enrol in a course or order a book on edgecoursebd.com or Rokomari with the coupon REVENGE (courses 40% off, books up to 20% off, 72-hour window). Free free guidelines are the low-commitment first step.

## Positioning

A trusted coaching-and-publisher team whose mentors come from BUET, DU and DMC. The page must make books as visible as courses.

## Operating Context

Facebook ad click, thumb on a small screen, often in sunlight, slow data. Decision happens in under a minute.

## Capabilities and Constraints

No invented proof: no student counts for courses, ratings, testimonials or "popular" badges. Book proof lines printed on QNA covers are the owner's own claims and may appear only on books. Outbound links open in a new tab. Link allowlist and tracking data attributes stay.

## Brand Commitments

The owner chose ink and saffron as the page palette and rejected the orange and green pairing of the two logos. The font is QNA Font (Kona). Bangla-first copy in a native HSC register.

## Evidence on Hand

Course posters (24), real QNA book covers (18), course prices, mentor institutions from the posters, seven free guideline channels, owner proof lines for books.

## Product Principles

One decision per screen. The coupon is a concrete object, not a banner. Courses and books are equal paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, 44px targets, 16px body text, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, content visible without script where possible.
