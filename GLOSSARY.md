# Glossary

**Offer**:
The whole coupon campaign: one coupon code, one deadline, and a discount for courses and for books.
_Avoid_: sale, promo, campaign (as a code name)

**Coupon code**:
The word the student types at checkout to get the discount, for example REVENGE.
_Avoid_: promo code, voucher

**Offer deadline** (`endsAt`):
The moment the Offer stops, in Bangladesh time (+06:00).
_Avoid_: expiry, end date

**Offer status**:
Whether the Offer is `running` (before the deadline) or `ended` (at the deadline and after). There is no "not started yet".
_Avoid_: state, phase

**Coupon price**:
A course's regular price after the course discount, rounded to whole taka.
_Avoid_: sale price, discounted price

**Up-to discount** ("পর্যন্ত"):
A book discount that is a ceiling: the coupon gives at most the stated percent, not always that much. Printed as "২০% পর্যন্ত".
_Avoid_: max discount, flat discount (a flat discount is the opposite)
